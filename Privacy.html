Privacy Policy

App: BTC Buy Sell Signals (package: com.btcsignal.app)
Last updated: 12 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the "BTC Buy Sell Signals" app ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. We keep it simple: the App does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your personal information.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or sell your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or payment information. There is no sign-up or login.

2. Data stored on your device

The App saves a few simple preferences (for example your last selected exchange, chart timeframe, and chart indicator settings) in your device's local browser storage. This data stays on your device only and is never sent to us. You can remove it by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App.

3. Market data and third-party services

To show prices and charts, the App requests public market data from cryptocurrency exchanges (such as Binance, Bybit, MEXC, WEEX, OKX, Bitget, KuCoin, and Gate). Some requests pass through a relay server we run on Cloudflare Workers, which only forwards public price requests and does not store them or ask for any personal data.

Like any internet service, these providers (the exchanges, Cloudflare, and GitHub Pages, which hosts the App) may technically receive your IP address and basic request details when you load data. Their handling of that data is governed by their own privacy policies. We do not receive or use that information.

4. Referral links

The App may contain referral (affiliate) links to cryptocurrency exchanges. If you open a link and register or trade on that exchange, we may receive a commission from the exchange at no extra cost to you. When you leave the App, the exchange's own terms and privacy policy apply. We do not receive your exchange account details.

5. Not financial advice

Signals, indicators, and prices shown in the App are for information and education only. They are not financial, investment, or trading advice. Cryptocurrency trading is risky and you can lose money. The App is not an exchange, does not hold funds, and does not execute trades. You are responsible for your own decisions.

6. Children

The App is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Security

The App does not handle passwords or API keys and does not store personal data on any server. Please never share your exchange passwords or API keys with anyone claiming to represent the App.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the date above updated.
